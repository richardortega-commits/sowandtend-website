# Sow & Tend Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [Month Day, Year]

Sow & Tend is operated by **[Full legal name of your LLC]** ("Sow & Tend," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the Sow & Tend iOS app handles information. It describes the current app; features marked as coming soon in the app are not active.

## Information you choose to enter

During setup, you may enter your first name and a U.S. ZIP code and select or confirm a USDA hardiness zone. You may also enter sunlight and soil conditions. The app uses this information to suggest a starter garden and growing guidance.

You can create gardens and beds, select plants, record planting methods and dates, manage tasks and supplies, write notes, and add garden photos and captions. These records and your app preferences are stored on your device. Photos you select from your library or take with your camera are copied into the app's local storage. You control whether to provide them and can remove individual content in the app.

If a ZIP code spans more than one hardiness zone, the app may ask for a street address to resolve the zone. The address is used for that lookup and is not saved to your garden profile. You do not have to provide an address, though the more precise lookup may then be unavailable.

## Online lookups and outside services

Some features require an internet connection and send limited lookup information to other services:

- **Hardiness zones:** The app sends your ZIP code or location coordinates derived from an address to a public ArcGIS Online hardiness-zone service.
- **Frost estimates:** Apple's geocoding service may process a ZIP code or an address to determine coordinates. The app sends coordinates to Open-Meteo to request historical weather data and estimate a last-frost date.
- **Plant images:** When you view plant information, the app may search Wikimedia Commons using the plant name and load an available image from Wikimedia's servers.

These services may receive technical information associated with a network request, such as your IP address, as well as the lookup values described above. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy practices. We do not send your garden notes or personal garden photos to these services for these lookups. The app does not need access to your device's live GPS location for its ZIP and address lookup.

## Notifications and device permissions

With your permission, Sow & Tend schedules gardening reminders as local notifications on your device. You can change reminder preferences in the app and revoke notification permission in iOS Settings. The app asks for camera or photo-library access only when you choose to add a photo, subject to the permission controls provided by iOS.

Text-message and email reminders are not active in the current app. If you enter an email address or phone number into an available preferences field, it is stored locally and is not used to deliver messages at this time.

## Accounts, advertising, and purchases

The current app works without an online account. Account creation, cloud synchronization, and account recovery are not active. The current app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK, and we do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Product and affiliate purchasing features are not active. If we add accounts, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, affiliate links, or message delivery later, we will update this policy before those features are introduced.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

Your garden profile, records, preferences, and photos are primarily kept in the app's storage on your device. They remain there until you delete them or remove the app, subject to any device backups you have enabled with Apple. The app's **Delete local data** control removes local garden records, associated app photos, profile data, and local account credentials. Some app preferences may also need to be changed individually in Settings or removed by deleting the app. Deleting data on your device does not delete records that an outside lookup service may retain under its own policy.

We do not currently maintain a Sow & Tend cloud account or server copy of your garden. If you need help with a privacy request, contact us at the address below. We will respond as required by applicable law.

## Security

We use the storage and permission features provided by iOS for app data, photos, and local credentials. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Protect your device with the security settings that work for you.

## Children's privacy

Sow & Tend is a general gardening app and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us through the app, contact us so we can review the concern.

## Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when app features or data practices change. We will post the revised version with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required by law.
